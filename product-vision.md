# Product Vision

**For** porting coordinators and UCaaS project managers
**who** track dozens of phone number ranges across spreadsheets, shared
drives and email threads with carriers,
**Porting Tracker is** a web application
**that** centralizes the state of every number range, from LOA submission
to cutover confirmation, and surfaces what is at risk before the deadline
passes.

**Unlike** a shared spreadsheet, it calculates regulatory deadlines on its
own, tells the coordinator what needs action today, and keeps an auditable
history of every status change.

## Product goals

1. **No range misses its regulatory window.** A porting date agreed with a
   carrier cannot be rescheduled without restarting a 45-day clock. The
   product exists to make a slipping range visible while there is still
   time to escalate.
2. **No LOA is rejected for a data error.** Invalid or duplicated numbers
   are the most common cause of rework. Validation happens before
   submission, not after the carrier replies.
3. **One shared source of truth.** The coordinator, the project manager
   and the voice engineer read the same state, instead of three
   spreadsheets that disagree.

## Guiding principles

- **Deadline-first.** Every screen answers "what do I act on today?"
  before it answers "what is the full list?".
- **Evidence attached.** A status is only as good as the document behind
  it, so files live on the range, not in an inbox.
- **Nothing silently overwritten.** Every change is attributable.
