# Sprint goals

## Sprint 1 — "A range exists and its numbers are trustworthy"
A coordinator can create a range, import its numbers from a CSV, and be
stopped by the product when the file has invalid or duplicated numbers.
At the end of this sprint the data the product holds can be trusted, which
everything else depends on.

Stories: US-01 to US-05
Points: 22

## Sprint 2 — "A range moves through the workflow and shouts when it slips"
A coordinator can advance a range through the porting workflow, record LOA
and FSED dates, see the deadline the product calculates, and get a visible
alert when a carrier misses its response window. Evidence is attached to
the range.
At the end of this sprint the product does the thing it exists for.

Stories: US-06 to US-11
Points: 24

## Sprint 3 — "The whole program is visible and reportable"
The team can see every range grouped by status, filter down to today's
work, open an action list, follow the cutover calendar, and export the
current view for the weekly report. Changes are attributable.
At the end of this sprint the product is usable by all three personas, not
only by the coordinator.

Stories: US-12 to US-21
Points: 26

## Stretch, only if a sprint closes early
US-18 comments and US-21 notifications. They improve the product but
nothing in the MVP depends on them.
