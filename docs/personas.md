# Personas

## Camila, Porting Coordinator — primary user, daily
Runs 40 or more ranges at the same time across a multi-city program. She is
the one who submits LOAs, chases carriers and books cutover windows.

- **Goal:** know what needs action today without reading forty rows.
- **Frustration:** she finds out a carrier never replied only when the
  cutover week arrives.
- **Success looks like:** she opens the app, sees three ranges flagged,
  escalates them, and closes the app in ten minutes.
- **Uses:** several times a day, on a laptop, often with the carrier on
  the phone.

## Ravi, Project Manager — weekly
Owns the program plan and reports to the client. He does not touch
individual numbers.

- **Goal:** report progress and flag risk before the client asks.
- **Frustration:** the numbers in his status report are copied by hand
  and are stale by the time he sends it.
- **Success looks like:** he exports the current view and the report
  writes itself.
- **Uses:** weekly, before the status meeting and the steering call.

## Jonas, Voice Engineer — per cutover window
Configures the platform and runs the technical validation on the night of
the cut.

- **Goal:** know exactly which ranges are cleared for his window and what
  was agreed with the carrier.
- **Frustration:** he is handed a spreadsheet tab at 22:00 and has to
  trust it.
- **Success looks like:** he opens the calendar view and sees his window
  with the confirmed ranges and the attached documents.
- **Uses:** a few times per window, sometimes on a phone from the site.
