# Success metrics

| Metric | How it is measured | Baseline (spreadsheet) | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time to spot a range at risk | Interview: how long between a carrier missing its SLA and the coordinator noticing | 3 to 7 days | under 1 day |
| LOAs submitted within the planned window | Ranges with submission date on or before plan / total | not measured | 100% |
| LOAs rejected for a data error | Rejections tagged as data error / total submitted | about 1 in 8 | 0 |
| Ranges ready for cutover without confirmed FSED | Count at the go/no-go checkpoint | occasional | 0, blocked by the product |
| Weekly report preparation time | Self-reported by the project manager | 45 to 60 min | under 10 min |
| Daily active use by coordinators | Sessions per working day per coordinator | n/a | at least 1 |

## Leading indicator for the team
Number of ranges whose status was last changed more than 7 days ago. A
growing number means the tool is being kept up to date by nobody, which is
the failure mode that killed the spreadsheet.
